About Your Specialist

With over a decade of hands-on experience navigating New York City's Department of Buildings (DOB) and Department of Housing Preservation & Development (HPD) systems, I help homeowners and property owners resolve violations, pass inspections, and stay ahead of code changes before they become costly problems.

I've guided hundreds of residential properties — from single-family brownstones to multi-unit buildings — through boiler and elevator inspections, facade safety filings (FISP/Local Law 11), fire safety compliance, and registration renewals. My approach is simple: clear communication, realistic timelines, and no surprises.

  • Licensed Property Compliance Consultant, State of New York
  • 10+ years resolving DOB & HPD violations
  • Familiar with FISP/Local Law 11, Local Law 87, and Local Law 97 filings

Forms & Services Offered

Whether you're facing an open violation or want to get ahead of your next inspection, here's how I can help.

Violation Remediation

Review, dispute, and resolve open DOB and HPD violations, including certification of correction filings.

Compliance Audits

A full property review against current NYC code requirements to catch issues before they become violations.

Facade & Local Law Filings

FISP/Local Law 11 facade filings, Local Law 87 energy audits, and Local Law 97 emissions reporting support.

Permit & Registration Filings

Preparation and submission of DOB permit applications, HPD property registrations, and renewal paperwork.

Inspection Preparation

On-site walkthroughs ahead of boiler, elevator, fire safety, and general property inspections.

Ongoing Compliance Monitoring

Scheduled check-ins and deadline tracking so renewals and filings never catch you off guard.
